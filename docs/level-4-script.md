# Level 4 — Delivery Attempt: full script (draft 1)

Dialogue and story script for Level 4. It follows the voice rules in `game-script.md` and the level plan in `level-4-plan.md`. Nothing here is in the game yet.

Open decisions the script has to pick a side on are marked **[D4]** (who is on comms), **[D5]** (what the socket flashes) and **[D6]** (the archive room). Each has a default in the script and an alternative at the end.

## Cast and voice (new for Level 4)

| Speaker | Who | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **BIX** | Maintenance technician. Playable. | Tired, deadpan, practical. Short lines. |
| **PACK** | Robot backpack companion. | Cheerful, literal, dry. Reports disasters as status updates. Names things. |
| **VELA** | Facility dispatcher AI (comms). | Flat and bureaucratic. In this level her messages get shorter the higher they climb. |
| **NIB** | Depot market manager. Small, four-armed, in an apron. | Serious production manager. Records every avoidable disaster and is never angry. |
| **CUSTOMS DESK** | Automated desk in the market. | Polite, procedural, obsessed with paperwork. |
| **COURIER** | Small delivery bot that steals the core in area 4. | Chirpy and fast. Says everything twice. |
| **COURIER PRIME** | The big courier unit that guards the lift (mini-boss). | Warm customer-service voice. Terrifying because it is so helpful. |
| **VENDOR** | Vending bots in the market. | Cheerful salespeople with nothing to sell. |
| **SYSTEM** | HUD messages, checkpoints, medals. | Plain and short. |

## Writing rules (from the main script, plus three for this level)

- Lines are about 70 characters, two short lines at most.
- One joke per line. Pack delivers it, Bix deflates it. VELA gives orders. Pack gives commentary.
- Hazard lines fire only after the player has seen the hazard once.
- Death lines are one sentence, never repeated back to back, and never blame the player harshly. In this level they arrive as **Nib's incident reports**.
- The core is called **Dennis** (Pack's choice). Bix never uses the name until the last scene.
- Pack's "Good news / Bad news" appears once at the opener and once in the finale (continuity rule).
- Nothing about BX-7 is ever explained. It is only ever shown.

## The five story lines that matter (for continuity)

1. **VELA softens**, then goes quiet. Level 2: she says nothing. Level 3: dismissive. Level 4: shorter and gentler, then "Please—" and static.
2. **Pack names things.** Dennis is the running gag; he is only ever "the core" to Bix until the last scene, when Bix says "Thanks, Dennis."
3. **BX-7 is only shown.** The customs log, the courier route, the crawlspace plaque and the work order all point at the same name and nobody explains it.
4. **Pack goes quiet twice** (the customs slip, the crawlspace plaque) and answers with one word at the end ("Yes."). It is never explained.
5. **The surface is not empty.** A lit window, a small drone, and daylight. The level cuts before Bix sees anything.

## Alternatives for the open decisions

**[D4] Who is on comms?**
- *Default (in the script):* VELA returns softer and fades out.
- *Alternative A, silence:* delete O1, O3 and 4.29; Pack says "VELA has left the call" at O2 and it stays true all level.
- *Alternative B, a new voice:* replace VELA with **LIFT ATTENDANT** ("Welcome to Transit. Please hold.") for O1, 4.29 and 5.27, and let VELA speak nowhere.

**[D5] What does the socket flash?**
- *Default:* AUTHORISED: BX-7, then "Display glitch." "Yeah."
- *Alternative A, Bix's own ID:* flash AUTHORISED: BX-7 / BIX, and Bix says nothing after "Yeah."
- *Alternative B, nothing:* no flash; 6.30 to 6.32 replaced by Pack: "Core seated. Lift accepts. Lift does not explain."

**[D6] All-12-cogs archive room?**
- *If yes:* add a small records room off the shaft foot with three terminals: a delivery log for Dennis, a courier retirement notice ("Courier Prime: retired, 11 years ago"), and a note: "BX-7 WILL RETURN." Pack: "That last one wasn't there before." *(Only if the player wants it; it does not change the ending.)*
- *If no:* the level ends as scripted.
