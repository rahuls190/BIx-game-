The four phases

#PhaseWhat happens
1PursuitShe follows the feed line, which runs where Bix is running. No pods yet. Learn her footfall tell and that she cannot be stopped.
2Alarm revealBix trips the dome alarm, the lights come up, and it turns out she was never chasing him. Pods go live from here.
3Awakened gridBait a trough, get clear, let her lean in. Her weight drops the column and the column becomes the route. Three times, three set-ups.
4Nesting bayLure her onto the bedding cart. It tips, she settles, the feed line stops. She is fed and put to bed, not beaten.

The Lure · the one new idea

  • E take a pod from a hopper, carry it, set it down
  • X (Red) throw it — it bursts into a feed scatter
  • Z (Blue) call an unburst pod back to your hand
  • Her rule: she always walks to the newest burst, never to Bix
  • Same buttons as Level 4's core, so nothing new to learn at the hardest moment
  • A lost pod is always replaceable — there is no state with no pod
  • The lure opens the route; it is never how you survive. Every chase can be outrun

Must be true when it ships

  • Every jump on the route simulated in the real engine, carrying a pod and empty-handed
  • From every checkpoint, with any columns already down, the exit is still reachable
  • A fallen column stays fallen through a respawn; a restart puts it back
  • Bix can never damage her, and she only hurts him by footfall, sweep or shockwave
  • 16 random 40-second runs at 30 / 60 / 120 / 240 fps stay finite and throw nothing
  • 32 carried cogs leave it locked; 33 open it

Look and sound

  • Warm, dusty, overgrown: grain haze, green columns, work lamps, timber and galvanised steel
  • Must not read as Level 5 — that one is cold storm-grey. Own deck and backdrop per area
  • She is warm amber and machine-white. Never red: red means danger, and she is not the danger
  • Flat side-on camera, semi-realistic metal, never cartoon
  • Sound: footfalls near and far, pod burst, trough bait, column groan, cart on rails, then silence
  • Art ships as WebP, whole level under 4 MB

The one decision left to you
